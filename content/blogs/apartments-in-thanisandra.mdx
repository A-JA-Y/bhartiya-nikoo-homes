Thanisandra is the Hebbal–Thanisandra belt — the corridor that Manyata Tech Park built and the airport road made permanent. That makes it one of North Bangalore's busiest apartment markets, and one of the easiest to get wrong if you compare brochures instead of fundamentals. This guide sets out what separates projects in Thanisandra from each other, with Nikoo Homes 8's figure for each.

## Why buyers look at Thanisandra

<a id="why"></a>

Three things drive demand here: the Manyata employment base, the airport corridor, and the Namma Metro Blue Line, with the Hebbal section targeted for June 2027 and Nagawara for March 2028. For Nikoo Homes 8 specifically, there is a fourth — [Bhartiya City](/bhartiya-city), five to seven minutes away.

## The honest risk: traffic

<a id="risk"></a>

Manyata is about 5.6 km away in a straight line — ten to fifteen minutes off peak, twenty-five to thirty at nine in the morning. Hebbal junction, the Outer Ring Road and Hennur Road all bottleneck at rush hour. The metro fixes much of that, but it is 2027 to 2028, not today. Buy for a 2028-onward commute, not a 2026 one. Distances and drive times from the gate to Manyata, Hebbal, the airport and the metro stations are on the [location](/location) page.
