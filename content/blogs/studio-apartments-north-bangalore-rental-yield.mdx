## Risks to underwrite

<a id="risks"></a>

- **Timeline:** the RERA-filed completion is December 2030 — there is no rent until handover.
- **Traffic:** Hebbal, the Outer Ring Road and Hennur Road congest badly; the Blue Line metro reaches Hebbal (June 2027) and Nagawara (March 2028) before handover, which should help rentability.
- **Supply:** confirm how many studios remain in the current release before you plan around one.
